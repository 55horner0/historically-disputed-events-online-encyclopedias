About MPropositionneur-V2

Zual/MPropositionneur-V2 is a compact language model built on Qwen3-0.6B and trained via knowledge distillation specifically for atomic proposition extraction by Luc Pommeret at LISN (CNRS).

It breaks complex sentences into semantically faithful, self-contained atomic propositions. In this project, it decomposes cross-lingual Wikipedia articles into atomic units to detect contradictory factual claims between languages.

Prompt format: <|im_start|>user Atomize: {text}<|im_end|> <|im_start|>assistant
Generated datasets:
